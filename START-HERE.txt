DJ LIBRARY — INTEL MAC / MACOS MONTEREY

1. Extract this ZIP in Downloads. Keep all of its files together.
2. Open Terminal and paste:

bash "$HOME/Downloads/DJ-Library/Install.command"

If you extracted it elsewhere, type bash followed by a space, drag Install.command
from Finder into Terminal, and press Return.

The installer checks its package, installs its private Python environment and
bundled Soulseek client, creates a Desktop launcher, and opens your browser.
It does not require the Codex app, Homebrew, Docker, or an administrator password.
Python 3.10+ must already be installed.

NEXT TIME
Double-click DJ Library.command on your Desktop. Keep its Terminal window open.
Press Control-C in Terminal to stop the app and its Soulseek client.

FIRST SCAN
Choose your audio folder in the browser and click Scan library. Read the report.
Open Mixed In Key using the button, analyze your source files, and enable writing
key, BPM and energy to their tags. Rescan after analysis.
Under Organize, preview the proposed folders, then copy and create a playlist.
Your original files remain in place. Allow space for the copied library.
Import the .m3u8 playlist in the organized folder into Rekordbox.
Cues, grids, playlists and existing Rekordbox references are not migrated.

SOULSEEK
slskd 0.24.5 is bundled and installed automatically. It uses .NET 8, compatible
with Monterey. Your Soulseek login is entered locally in the Soulseek tab;
the password is passed to the child process and not saved by the organizer.
Quit your other Soulseek client first if it uses the same account.
Search an artist/track, refresh results, select a version, and queue the download.
Completed downloads are saved under ~/Music/DJ Inbox.
Analyze them with Mixed In Key, then scan the inbox and organize them.
Only obtain music you have permission to download.
macOS may require you to approve the downloaded executable in Security & Privacy.
The installer does not disable Gatekeeper or remove quarantine protections.

WHAT IS AND ISN'T AUTOMATED
Installed: audio-tag reader, browser app, native Intel slskd client, launchers.
Automated: tag scanning, exact-duplicate detection, folder planning, copying,
copy verification, playlist export, slskd setup/startup/search/download queues.
Manual: first launch and macOS permissions, Soulseek login, selecting files
to download, Mixed In Key analysis/tag writing, Rekordbox playlist import.
Not implemented: Spotify/SoundCloud account syncing or streaming audio extraction,
automatic genre classification, automated Mixed In Key GUI control, automatic
selection of the correct extended mix, or importing previous ChatGPT chats.
Find Music provides search links and a local wishlist using your artist references.

VALIDATION
Tests run on Linux with generated tagged audio and the real slskd HTTP API.
The Intel Mac package is checked against official published download hashes.
Actual execution on Monterey, Mixed In Key integration, Rekordbox import and
Soulseek account login/transfers require validation on your Mac.

PRIVACY / DATA
The organizer binds its HTTP interface to 127.0.0.1 with a per-session token.
slskd's web API is also bound to 127.0.0.1 with generated authentication.
The Soulseek peer listener is a separate protocol connection on port 50319.
No music folders are automatically shared.
Library reports and copy journals:
~/Library/Application Support/DJ Library
App files:
~/Applications/DJ Library
No music or credentials are sent to ChatGPT by this app.
